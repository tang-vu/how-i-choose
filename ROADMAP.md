# Roadmap

How I Choose is an open alpha. The judged core is intentionally narrow: self-authored adult profiles, low-stakes communication rehearsal, local persistence, and an owner-controlled WebMCP layer.

## Alpha release — current

- deterministic partner-turn validation and partner-adherence reporting;
- explicit semantic signal board with persistent Pause and Stop;
- human-only and agent rehearsal modes sharing the same domain services;
- revision-safe IndexedDB persistence, history, import/export, and print;
- staged protocol suggestions with provenance and per-item owner review;
- derivation-checked support guides with draft watermarking;
- eight scoped imperative WebMCP Site tools;
- synthetic Maya judge path, machine-readable evals, and accessibility preferences.

## Next: co-design and evidence

- establish a compensated community advisory group with release-blocking authority;
- run accessible formative research across AAC, text-first, processing-time, sensory, and episodic-speech needs;
- complete independent accessibility, privacy, security, and harm reviews;
- test disclosure language, stale-revision recovery, and partner-report framing;
- publish findings, disagreements, and resulting product changes.

## Then: interoperability and resilience

- design migration-safe schema evolution after community review;
- add richer, user-authored signal representations without inferring meaning;
- improve backup/restore guidance and storage-loss recovery;
- replace static-export inline CSP allowances with a tested hash/nonce strategy;
- broaden browser and assistive-technology coverage;
- explore localization only with compensated language-community ownership.

## Deliberately deferred

Accounts, cloud sync, supporter collaboration, healthcare/EHR integration, speech recognition, transcription, emotion detection, crisis features, medical templates, public sharing, signatures, payments, organization administration, an embedded model, and native/PWA packaging are not alpha roadmap commitments. They require separate evidence, governance, and safety justification.

Organizational or healthcare exploration cannot begin until the gates in [CO_DESIGN.md](CO_DESIGN.md) are satisfied.
