# Co-design commitment

## Honest alpha status

This alpha has not yet been co-designed, usability-tested, or validated with the disabled adults and communication communities it intends to support. The Maya profile and all challenge data are synthetic. Automated accessibility engineering and review by the project team are not substitutes for lived-experience leadership.

No claim should imply that the product works for every disability, communication method, culture, language, device, support relationship, or context.

## Compensated participation plan

Before organizational or healthcare exploration:

1. form a compensated advisory group with adults who use AAC, experience episodic speech, need processing time, use sign/gesture or text-first communication, and have varied cognitive/sensory access needs;
2. pay participants for preparation, sessions, asynchronous feedback, access-support labor, and governance work at transparent professional rates;
3. reimburse access costs, transport, connectivity, personal-assistant time, interpreters, captioning, and preferred communication support;
4. offer multiple participation modes, flexible pacing, plain-language materials, advance agendas, breaks, and withdrawal without penalty;
5. avoid collecting diagnoses unless participants explicitly decide they are relevant to research goals;
6. publish what changed, what was rejected, unresolved disagreement, and how participant authority affected release decisions.

## Research questions

- Are signal meanings, labels, sizes, and partner actions understandable across access methods?
- Does the person feel in control of start, Pause, Stop, review, and ratification?
- Do revision and stale-agent messages make recovery understandable without blame?
- Are disclosure controls specific enough and easy to audit?
- Does the partner report avoid grading, pathologizing, or pressuring the person?
- Can people distinguish practice, documentation, and guide derivation from high-stakes decision processes?
- Do print and JSON workflows create new privacy or support burdens?
- What communication channels and signal representations are missing?

## Governance

Create a paid community governance group with authority to block harmful templates, language, release claims, and deployments. Establish conflict-of-interest disclosure, incident review, change logs, accessible feedback routes, data-retention rules, and a process for removing harmful features—not only adding features.

Research plans and consent for research participation must be separate from the product's communication-practice workflow.

## Gates before organizational or healthcare use

- compensated multi-round co-design completed;
- documented accessibility evaluation with representative assistive technology;
- independent privacy/security and harm review;
- validation of communication protocol fidelity in intended contexts;
- clear operator training, escalation, and incident governance;
- jurisdiction-specific legal and clinical review without presenting the app as authorization or decision evidence;
- evidence that partners use the guide to ask the person directly rather than substitute for them;
- a sustainable maintenance, support, and vulnerability-response plan.

Until those gates are met, keep use personal, self-authored, synthetic or low-stakes, and clearly labeled open alpha.
